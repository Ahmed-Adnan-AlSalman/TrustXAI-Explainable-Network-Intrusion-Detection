
Discussion

The experimental results demonstrate that both Random Forest and XGBoost
achieved excellent performance in multiclass network intrusion detection.

Although Random Forest achieved high classification accuracy, XGBoost
consistently outperformed it across all evaluation metrics.

The superior performance of XGBoost can be attributed to its gradient
boosting mechanism, which effectively captures complex nonlinear
relationships between network traffic features.

Furthermore, the Explainable AI techniques (SHAP and LIME) confirmed that
the model decisions were mainly influenced by meaningful network traffic
features rather than random attributes.

This increases the transparency and trustworthiness of the proposed
intrusion detection system.
